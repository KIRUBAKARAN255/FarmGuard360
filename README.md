Excited to share FARMGUARD 360: An AI and IoT-Based Smart Biosecurity Farm & Livestock Monitoring Dashboard, developed as part of our ADI1201 – Data Exploration and Visualization work at M. Kumarasamy College of Engineering, Karur.

Key highlights of what we built:
• Data Refinery & Preprocessing: Cleaned and structured 1,103 raw shift records into 1,095 validated seasonal records (365 days across 3 shifts) in Python using pandas and numpy, resolving missing entries, duplicates, and out-of-range sensor readings.
• Exploratory Data Analysis & Visualization: Generated diagnostic charts in matplotlib and analyzed seasonal rest baselines (13.5s summer limit) alongside safety-suit compliance (>= 32.0% threshold).
• Connected Biosecurity System: Integrated edge monitoring logic (ESP32, OpenCV) with dashboard visualization and structured audit logging.
